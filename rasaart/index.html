Features Tailored for Reflection

🎨

Adaptive Drawing Canvas

Express yourself with flexible brush options, adjustable stroke widths, and dynamic adaptive colors. Simple, elegant, and distraction-free.

📖

Story Prompt Library

Explore curated drawing epics (like the Ramayana), custom collections, and character sections designed to inspire your drawings and thoughts.

🧘

Mindful Reflection

Conclude every drawing session with gentle, prompt-driven reflection questions and notes. Save your thoughts alongside your art.

Your Journey & Streaks

Track your consistency with daily streaks, view your activity calendar heatmap, and browse a chronological timeline of your creative milestones.

100% Private & Local

All drawings, reflections, and notes stay safely on your device using Apple SwiftData. No accounts, no cloud sync, and absolute privacy.

Offline First

Rasa Art functions fully offline. Prompts, drawing tools, and local storage work without any internet connection required.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Rasa Art is built with a commitment to absolute privacy. As an offline-first application, the philosophy of Rasa Art is simple: your data belongs to you, and it should remain under your control on your own physical hardware.

1. Data Storage & On-Device Security

Rasa Art does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to external servers. All drawings, canvas sketches, notes, reflection answers, streaks, activity heatmap data, and history milestones are saved locally on your iOS/iPadOS device using Apple SwiftData. These records stay on your device at all times and are never uploaded to any cloud infrastructure or accessed by the developer.

2. No Accounts Required

You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information (such as your name, email, or telephone number) to use Rasa Art. You can start drawing and reflecting immediately after download.

3. StoreKit & Purchase Processing

Rasa Art offers an optional lifetime Premium purchase (Non-Consumable in-app purchase) to unlock additional content. Transactions are managed entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework. Payment details, card information, billing addresses, and transactions are processed securely by Apple. The developer of Rasa Art does not collect, receive, or store any of your financial information or payment transactions.

4. Analytics, Telemetry & Tracking

Rasa Art does not contain any third-party SDKs, analytics packages, telemetry trackers, or advertisement networks. We do not track your location, your app usage frequency, your drawing habits, or any other behaviors. The app functions entirely without telemetry.

5. Offline Functionality & Third-Party SDKs

All core prompt databases, story libraries, and services are bundled directly with the application binary. Rasa Art works completely offline, eliminating any background network request dependencies. No third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) are embedded in the app.

6. Device Permissions

Rasa Art operates without requiring any sensitive system permissions, such as location services, contacts, camera, or microphone access. Drawing capabilities use standard sandbox file access to write and read your local SwiftData store.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Since Rasa Art collects no contact information, we recommend checking this page periodically for updates.

8. Developer Contact

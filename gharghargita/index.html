Bring the Gita Home

GharGharGita is designed to make the timeless teachings of the Bhagavad Gita easily accessible, readable, and resonant in your daily modern life.

Available on App Store

Features Tailored for Devotion

📖

Sacred Verse Reading

Read all 18 chapters and 700 verses of the Bhagavad Gita with clear, beautiful Sanskrit typography, transliterations, and translations.

🎧

Audio Recitations

Listen to peaceful, authentic audio recitations of the verses to aid your pronunciation, memorization, and daily meditation.

✍️

Bookmarks & Notes

Save your favorite verses and write down your personal reflections or realizations on each teaching as you study.

🧘

Mindful & Offline

No internet connection or account signup required. Keep your study peaceful, private, and distraction-free on your iOS device.

About Per Minute Content Reader

Per Minute Content Reader is designed to help you read faster, retain more, and build consistent reading habits without sacrificing privacy. Import your own documents, practice with Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP) accelerated reading, take reading sprints, and measure your comprehension—all in an offline-first, account-free environment where your files remain strictly on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported?
Per Minute Content Reader supports PDF (.pdf), EPUB (.epub), and Plain Text (.txt) documents. You can import documents directly from your device storage or files app using the native system document picker.
What is RSVP reading and how does it work?
RSVP stands for Rapid Serial Visual Presentation. It displays words one by one in a fixed focal position at a controlled speed (measured in words per minute). By eliminating the need for your eyes to scan back and forth across lines of text, RSVP minimizes physical eye strain and reduces subvocalization, allowing for faster reading speeds.
How does the 7-day local trial work?
Per Minute Content Reader provides a 7-day local in-app trial for RSVP accelerated reading and Training Sprints. This trial:
  • Is stored locally on your device and requires no account or credit card.
  • Starts strictly upon your first activation of an RSVP session or Training Sprint.
  • Does not start upon app installation, opening the paywall screen, standard reading, or baseline assessment.
  • Runs for 7 consecutive local calendar days. It is not an App Store or Google Play subscription trial.
What Premium membership options are available?
After your 7-day local trial expires, you can unlock unlimited RSVP reading, Training Sprints, and On-Device Intelligence through:
  • Monthly Subscription: Renews monthly.
  • Annual Subscription: Renews annually and is featured as our Best Value option.
  • Lifetime Purchase: A one-time payment that unlocks Premium permanently.
Exact prices are set by Apple App Store or Google Play in your local currency.
How do I restore my purchases on a new or second device?
Open Per Minute Content Reader, navigate to the Premium screen, and tap "Restore Purchases." As long as your device is signed in to the same Apple ID (on iOS) or Google account (on Android) used for the original purchase, your membership will activate immediately without any additional charge.
How do I cancel an active subscription?
Subscriptions are billed through your platform account:
  • On iOS: Open device Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, select Per Minute Content Reader, and tap Cancel Subscription.
  • On Android: Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon, go to Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, select the app, and tap Cancel.
You retain full access until the end of the paid billing period.
Where are my documents and reading data stored?
All imported PDF, EPUB, and TXT documents, reading positions, speed metrics, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device within the app's secure local sandbox. We do not operate a remote server or cloud database, and your documents are never uploaded to the developer.
How does On-Device Intelligence work?
The app's intelligence tools run locally on your device's processor using on-device models. There is no cloud AI server, and your document text is never sent to external AI APIs. Performance and inference speed depend on your physical device hardware capabilities.
How do I delete a document or reset my data?
You can delete any imported document directly from your library list inside the app. Deleting a document removes its file and cached contents from local storage. To reset all application data, you can delete the app from your device, which clears the local sandbox.
What should I do if a PDF or EPUB fails to open?
Ensure the file is not password-protected or encrypted with proprietary DRM (Digital Rights Management). For very large PDF files with heavy embedded imagery, ensure your device has sufficient free memory. If a plain text file has strange characters, verify that it is saved in UTF-8 encoding.
